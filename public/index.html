Liquidity Atlas

Graph-based liquidity intelligence for Monad. Value is modeled as water flowing between containers.

  • Nodes — assets, wallets, pools, vaults, bridges, protocols, validators
  • Edges — transfers, ownership, collateral, staking, bridging, dependencies
  • Snapshots — immutable, content-addressed points in time
  • Events — observed movements that drive attribution
